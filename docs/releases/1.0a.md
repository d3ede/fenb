# FENB 1.0a

FENB is a graphics mod for Flyff, inspired by ENBSeries. This release brings
all changes since 0.7a together under public version **1.0a**.

## Profiles

The built-in selector now contains five profiles in this order:

1. **Default** keeps the shipped image settings.
2. **Default (High-end PC)** enables surface reflections, local lighting,
   detailed grass, particle processing and detailed effect quality, with
   full-resolution 48-step water SSR and subtle caustics.
3. **Default (Mid-range PC)** keeps SSAO enabled and adds simple grass.
4. **Default (Low-end PC)** retains its previous settings, with SSAO disabled.
5. **Default (Potato)** retains its previous lightweight settings, including
   FXAA and reduced water and scene effects.

High-end PC and Mid-range PC both enable surface appearance at **0.85**,
snow appearance at **0.9**, and microcontrast with detail strength **0.45**
and texture contrast **0.65**. The water and color variants have been removed
from the built-in selector. Custom profiles remain available. All five names
are translated into English, Russian, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese;
loading profiles preserves the selected language and client controls.

## Vegetation and winter scenery

- Added configurable simple and detailed grass, with quality, distance,
  density, shape, wind and color controls. Detailed blades can adapt to the
  ground color; Simple uses the supplied mesh and seasonal atlases.
- Enabled custom grass replaces recognized native grass. Coverage follows
  terrain slopes and blended ground layers, excludes paving and water,
  and tapers with distance from the character.
- Added independent bark and foliage controls for recognized Flaris and
  Azria tree families.
- Added grouped snow controls for grass, crowns, terrain and scenery.
  Flaris winter coverage includes ground, paths, hedges, roofs and the
  timber house; recognition remains limited to the material catalog.
- Added material surface shading, snow appearance and optional image
  microcontrast in the Materials tab.

## Reflections, particles and optimization

- Added optional rain puddles with gradual filling and drying, material
  coverage controls, sky and screen-space reflections, and rain rings.
- Added smooth refinement glow and separate armor, weapon, board trail and
  board particle colors, with five board trail styles and five particle
  shapes. Improved trail continuity and blending with grass.
- Added shop-sign range, independent native effect filters, and separate
  visibility and draw-distance controls for players, monsters and pets.
- Added resource-lookup caching with lifecycle invalidation and reorganized
  panel navigation. Native rendering state and HUD preservation remain
  covered by the test suite.

## Install or update

Download **FENB-1.0a-win32.zip**. Close the game and copy `d3d9.dll` beside
`Neuz.exe`, replacing `LICENSES.txt` with the supplied copy. New installations
also need the included `FENB.ini`; existing users should keep their own
configuration and `FENB-presets/`. Open the panel with **Shift + Enter**.

Published versions **0.5a through 0.7a** can use **FENB updates** in the panel's
**…** menu. Versions **0.4a and earlier** need a manual DLL replacement.
Local development builds numbered **1.1 through 1.3** also need one manual
installation of 1.0a because their development version numbers compare as
newer. Their settings and custom profiles can be retained.

The release includes **FENB-update.json** for automatic updates and a
**.sha256** checksum for manual verification. `INSTALL.txt` includes English
and Russian instructions; `LICENSES.txt` preserves FENB, dependency and
bundled artwork notices. The published DLL has diagnostics disabled.

Requires Windows and a compatible **32-bit DirectX 9 Flyff client**. Native
adapters currently target supported **Homesickness Flyff** layouts; other
clients remain unverified. Depth-based effects need a supported NVIDIA
driver depth-copy path. Screen-space reflections require visible scene
content, and puddles cannot detect shelter. See the [README](../../README.md)
and [rendering notes](../RENDERING.md) for details.
