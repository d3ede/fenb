Bundled game artwork

The Simple grass mesh and its normal and winter atlases were supplied by
the project owner and identified in the source archive as Flyff Universe
game assets. Their original licensing is unchanged. Inclusion in FENB
does not assign a new license to this third-party artwork.

Tree appearance assets retain the original game's geometry and UV layout.
Source tree artwork remains attributable to its original owners. FENB's
replacement assets do not grant a new license to underlying game artwork.

The MIT license for FENB code does not relicense third-party game artwork.
Source and provenance notes are preserved in assets/grass/universe/README.md,
assets/grass/universe/provenance.json and assets/trees/README.md in the
FENB source repository: https://github.com/d3ede/fenb
